# Product Guidelines

## Brand Character

Flowly is calm, capable, and optimistic. It makes work feel less overwhelming without becoming overly playful or casual.

## Voice and Tone

- Clear, direct, and encouraging
- Short sentences over feature-heavy jargon
- Confident but never pushy
- Focused on outcomes: clarity, momentum, and calmer workdays

## UX Principles

1. Make the next action obvious.
2. Use generous space to reduce cognitive load.
3. Prioritize scanability over decoration.
4. Keep the primary CTA visible and specific.
5. Present pricing transparently, with comparable details in aligned rows.

## Visual Direction

- Warm, light editorial surfaces with a single confident accent
- Strong typographic hierarchy and restrained interface detail
- Product imagery that shows organized work in context
- Responsive layouts that preserve clarity at every viewport

## Accessibility

- Maintain readable contrast and visible keyboard focus
- Use semantic headings, navigation, sections, tables, and buttons/links
- Keep touch targets at least 44px where practical
- Respect reduced-motion preferences
